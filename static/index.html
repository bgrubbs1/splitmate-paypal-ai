        How SplitMate uses AI + PayPal

        Two AI steps, one payment platform, no hand-waving.

        ✦

        AI parsing

        An OpenAI-compatible model turns free text into a strict JSON plan: participants, items, amounts, who paid, and participation caveats. A deterministic rule-based parser handles offline mode and any AI failure.

        ≈

        Fair-split math

        Each item is allocated in integer cents with largest-remainder rounding, so totals always reconcile to the penny. Partial attendance produces per-item exclusions, and netting means one payment per debtor.

        ◇

        PayPal Orders v2

        OAuth2 client-credentials auth, one order per debtor via /v2/checkout/orders, hosted checkout links, then capture once approved. Statuses are tracked per share, with AI-drafted reminders.

        ▦

        AG Grid review

        AI output is checked before money moves. An AG Grid matrix shows each person's exact share of each item, lets you correct the AI in place, and shows whether the totals reconcile. A second grid is the payment ledger.

        Mock mode (default)

        Deterministic offline PayPal + AI stand-ins. Perfect for judges: python3 app.py and click through the whole flow with no accounts or network.

        Real sandbox mode

        Add PAYPAL_CLIENT_ID / PAYPAL_CLIENT_SECRET (and an LLM key) to .env. SplitMate then creates real Orders v2 orders in your PayPal sandbox and redirects to PayPal's sandbox checkout.
